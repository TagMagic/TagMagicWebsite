About

Our Mission

TagMagicAPI exists to make audio metadata simple, fast, and reliable. We built it so creators, developers, and platforms can keep their libraries clean and consistent without dealing with slow tools or manual tagging

Who We Serve

TagMagicAPI is designed for developers, DJs, radio stations, podcast platforms, archivists, media servers, automation builders, and anyone who needs accurate metadata at scale. If clean tags matter to your workflow, TagMagicAPI is built for you

Why TagMagicAPI

TagMagicAPI delivers clean metadata in milliseconds, works with all major audio formats, returns simple JSON, and never stores your files. It’s fast, predictable, automation‑ready, and built for real‑world audio workflows
